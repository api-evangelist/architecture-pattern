Software engineers draw on many kinds of reusable knowledge, including principles, practices, techniques, patterns, designs, methods, rules, reference architectures, and implementation approaches. Lots of people write about patterns, myself included. I’ve noticed, however, that like many concepts in software engineering, the concept of a pattern has suffered from semantic diffusion .
